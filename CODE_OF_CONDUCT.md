# Code of conduct

ThermoTune follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful, assume good intent, welcome newcomers, and keep criticism about the
work rather than the person. Harassment, insults, and personal or political attacks aren't
welcome in issues, pull requests, or any other project space.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour in this project's spaces concerns you, email the maintainer,
[@armendu](https://github.com/armendu), at the address on their GitHub profile. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments, commits, issues, or other contributions
that break this code of conduct, and may ban someone from the project temporarily or
permanently.
